# Stitch Beats — ready-to-paste (one run each)

Run these one at a time in Stitch (Web). Each is self-contained. Generate, save the
result, move to the next. Together they form the storyboard for the scroll-told
story. Beats 1–3 are dark/nocturnal, 4–5 warm interior, 6 dark again, 7 dreamlike,
8 warm and resolved.

Tip: if any beat feels templated, add "less website, more film title sequence /
luxury campaign; remove anything that looks like a startup."

---

## Shared preamble (already inside each beat below — no need to add)

> Design one beat of an immersive, cinematic, scroll-told story for an
> ultra-exclusive, by-invitation private members' club called "Sodalis." The whole
> website is a single narrative journey about being secretly let in: it opens on a
> key, the key turns as you scroll, a door opens, and you are admitted into a
> private world. High-end, mysterious, ambitiously art-directed — like a film title
> sequence or a luxury fragrance campaign, NOT a normal website. Bold depth,
> dimensionality, lighting and atmosphere; make it feel three-dimensional and alive
> even as a still frame. Hold true: discreet, expensive, quiet, powerful; strong
> depth and material (brass, glass, stone, warm light, shadow); restraint and
> negative space; very few words; the only call to action, only at the very end, is
> requesting an introduction — never "sign up," pricing, tiers, amenity lists, or
> stock photos of people. Confident typography. Make a desktop frame and a matching
> mobile frame.

## BEAT 1 — The Key  ✅ (you've generated this)

Design THIS beat: **The Key — near-total darkness with a single ornate brass key
suspended in a shaft of warm light, slowly rotating; a faint "Scroll to enter" hint
near the bottom; almost nothing else.**

---

## BEAT 2 — The Turn

(Paste the shared preamble, then:)

Design THIS beat: **The Turn — the same brass key now meeting an ornate keyhole in
deep shadow, caught mid-turn; a thin seam of warm golden light beginning to bleed
through from the other side, hinting at a space beyond. Tense, anticipatory, dark.
No brand name yet, almost no text.**

---

## BEAT 3 — The Door Opens

Design THIS beat: **The Door Opens — a heavy door swinging open / dissolving as warm
golden light floods toward the viewer from within. The brand name "Sodalis" is
revealed for the first time, set large like an engraving over the entrance. This is
the moment of being let in: dramatic, luminous, still restrained. Dark frame giving
way to warm light.**

---

## BEAT 4 — The Threshold (The Idea)

Design THIS beat: **The Threshold — we have crossed over into a calm, warm interior
bathed in candlelight and bone-colored light. Vast space, brass accents, an elegant
high-contrast serif. A short line, oblique and confident: "Some rooms are known by
who is not in them." Quiet, expensive, lots of negative space.**

---

## BEAT 5 — The One Number

Design THIS beat: **The One Number — warm interior, even more restrained. A large
serif line, "One number, answered.", with a short supporting line beneath it: "A
stay arranged before the call ends. Whatever is required, quietly arranged." Maybe a
single faint row of three serif words separated by small brass dots: Stays ·
Journeys · The unspoken. No icons, no lists.**

---

## BEAT 6 — Discretion

Design THIS beat: **Discretion — the deepest, most private room. Pull back into
shadow: darker, hushed, almost empty. A single serif line in low warm light:
"Discretion is not a courtesy here. It is the product." Enormous negative space,
secrecy, weight.**

---

## BEAT 7 — In Time, A Place

Design THIS beat: **In Time, A Place — dreamlike and distant. A soft-focus,
barely-there suggestion of a far-off building or a hill landscape glimpsed through
haze or a window, never literal or detailed. One quiet serif line: "In time, a
place. For now, a number that always answers." Atmospheric, faded, almost a memory.**

---

## BEAT 8 — The Invitation

Design THIS beat: **The Invitation — calm, warm, resolved. A quiet minimal form on
bone/candlelit background with underline-only fields (no boxes): Full name, Email,
"The member who referred you", and an optional Message. One understated outline
button: "Request an introduction". A small serif heading above it: "Request an
introduction." No pricing, no tiers. Generous spacing, restrained.**
